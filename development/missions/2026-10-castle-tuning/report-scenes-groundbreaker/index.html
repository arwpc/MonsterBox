The wiper motor (part 1) is listed broken; jaw animation off; no servo. poses.json is {"characterId":5,"poses":[],"templates":{}} on purpose: the validator rejects a pose with no parts (sceneValidator.js:340) and a pose may hold only servo/motor/linear_actuator/light/ led parts; his only motor is broken (a warning the brief treats as an error).
