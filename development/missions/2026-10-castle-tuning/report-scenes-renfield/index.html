    Timing model. A GPIO servo step blocks max(150, duration) ms and each starts its own servo_cli.py (≈0.38 s Python+lgpio start-up on Orlok's Pi 4B); gen.py counts 400 ms per stroke (STROKE_OH), so "a 250 ms stroke every 650 ms" is back-to-back 250 ms servo steps. To write under a line, the sayThis is concurrent and the strokes follow it; a trailing wait sized for a fast 200 ms start-up (STROKE_OH_MIN) keeps lines from overlapping. Pen poses skip easing on purpose (transitionDurationMs: 200: above 250 ms each eased frame of a GPIO servo becomes its own blocking call); fast strokes are servo steps of 150–400 ms, poses supply held positions and the motor.
