started, the other had finished its edits and died before returning. **Their file edits survived on disk
and were salvaged by inspecting `git status`, not by waiting.**

  rather than digging on."*

- Do mechanical greps/edits INLINE — your own tool results are checkpointed permanently.
- **Never tell an agent to "ignore a system reminder"** — a safety classifier blocks the run. State the
  verified fact instead ("the connector is authorized; the lead has made many successful calls this
